## How it works

As a seller, you manage everything from your dashboard: sync products from Shopee, assign redemption links, and boost visibility. When a buyer purchases from your shop, they visit a simple public portal, enter their order number, and instantly receive the correct download or access link. The platform handles the connection between your Shopee shop and your buyers automatically.
