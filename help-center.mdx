## Quick Answers

The most common buyer questions:

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How do I get my redeem link?">

  Enter your Shopee order ID on the public redemption portal. The platform validates your order and displays your redemption links instantly. See our [Get Redeem Link FAQ](/help-center/faq/get-redeem-link) for details.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="My redeem link isn't working">

  This usually means the order is not yet eligible, or the seller hasn't configured a redemption URL for that product. See our [Redeem Link Not Working guide](/help-center/troubleshooting/redeem-link-not-working) for troubleshooting steps.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="How do I connect my Shopee shop?">

  Sellers connect their shop through the admin dashboard by creating a shop with Shopee partner credentials and completing the OAuth authorization. See our [Link Shopee Account guide](/help-center/guides/link-shopee-account) for the full walkthrough.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
