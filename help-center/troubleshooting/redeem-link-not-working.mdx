---
title: Redeem Link Not Working
description: What to do if your redeem link is invalid or expired.
---

## Symptoms

- Link shows "Invalid or expired"
- Cannot access documentation

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify Order ID">
    Make sure your Shopee order ID is correct (no spaces, copy from Shopee invoice).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Again">
    Visit https://wallyandcody.com/redeem and enter the order ID again to get a new link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Expiry">
    Links are valid for 24 hours; if expired, you must generate a fresh link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Need help?

Contact support if problems persist.
