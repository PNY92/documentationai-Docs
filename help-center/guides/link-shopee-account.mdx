title: Link Your Shopee Account

---

Connecting your Shopee shop allows the platform to sync products, manage redemption URLs, and validate buyer orders. This guide walks through creating a shop and completing the Shopee OAuth authorization.

## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before you begin, you need:

- An admin account on the platform
- Your Shopee Open Platform partner ID, partner key, and shop ID
- The Shopee redirect URI configured in your backend environment

</Callout>

## Linking steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to the Admin Dashboard" icon="log-in">

    Open the admin dashboard in your browser and log in with your admin credentials.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Shop" icon="store">

    Navigate to Shop Management and create a new shop. Enter your Shopee partner ID, partner key, and shop ID. Associate the shop with your seller account.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Authorization URL" icon="link">

    From the shop's token management section, request a Shopee authorization URL. This generates a URL that redirects you to Shopee's consent screen.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in Shopee" icon="check-circle">

    Open the authorization URL in your browser. Log in to Shopee and approve the platform's access request. Shopee will redirect back to the platform's callback URL.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Token Health" icon="activity">

    After the redirect completes, check the token health for your shop. This confirms that access and refresh tokens have been stored and are valid.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">

Your shop is now connected. You can sync products, configure redemption URLs, and the background worker will automatically refresh tokens before they expire.

</Callout>

## Token auto-refresh

Once authorized, the background worker scans for expiring tokens every 5 minutes and refreshes them automatically. You do not need to manually refresh tokens unless the worker is not running.

<Callout kind="info">

If `TOKEN_REFRESH_WORKER` is not set to `true` in the backend environment, tokens will expire and manual refresh will be required. See the [deployment guide](/configuration) for worker configuration.

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

If the OAuth flow fails, check the following:

- Verify `SHOPEE_REDIRECT_URI` in `backend/.env` matches the callback URL registered in Shopee.
- Check that the Shopee partner credentials are correct.
- Ensure the admin dashboard origin is in `ALLOWED_CORS_HOSTNAMES`.
- Check backend logs for OAuth callback errors.

See the [seller shop management guide](/seller-shop-management) for the full API guide and [configuration guide](/configuration) for environment settings.

</Callout>
