## Overview

Linking your Shopee account to Wally And Cody streamlines redeem link requests.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account Settings">
    Log in at [wallyandcody.com](https://wallyandcody.com), then click your profile and select "Settings".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose 'Link Shopee'">
    Click the 'Link Shopee Account' button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Follow the Shopee OAuth prompts and authorize Wally And Cody.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done!">
    Your Shopee orders will now be available for quick redeem link generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
