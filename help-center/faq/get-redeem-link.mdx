## Get your redeem links

Enter your Shopee order ID on the public redemption portal, and the platform returns your redemption links instantly. No account or login is required.

<Steps>
<Step title="Find Your Order ID" icon="search">

Log in to Shopee, go to **My Purchases**, and copy your order ID. The order ID is a unique identifier for your purchase.

</Step>

<Step title="Visit the Redemption Portal" icon="external-link">

Open the [public redemption portal](https://wallyandcody.com/redeem) in your browser. This is the buyer-facing web app provided by the seller.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter Your Order ID" icon="edit-3">

Paste your Shopee order ID into the input field and submit. The platform validates your order through Shopee and resolves the matching redemption links.

</Step>

<Step title="Receive Your Links" icon="check-circle">

The portal displays your redemption links for each item in your order. If a product has multiple links, you will see a directory page with all available options.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Your order must be in one of these statuses to qualify for redemption: `READY_TO_SHIP`, `PROCESSED`, `RETRY_SHIP`, `SHIPPED`, `TO_CONFIRM_RECEIVE`, or `COMPLETED`. Orders that are still pending or cancelled will not return redemption links.

</Callout>

<ExpandableGroup>
<Expandable title="What if I see CONTACT_SELLER_REQUIRED?">

This means the seller has not configured a redemption URL for the item you purchased. Contact the seller directly and ask them to set up the redemption link for that product.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if I get a rate limit error?">

The platform allows 20 redemption requests per IP address per minute. Wait one minute and try again. If the problem persists, try from a different network or device.

</Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

<Callout kind="tip">

If your redemption link is not working, follow the [redeem link troubleshooting steps](/help-center/troubleshooting/redeem-link-not-working).

</Callout>
