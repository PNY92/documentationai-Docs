## Product boosting

Product boosting increases your product visibility on Shopee. This guide walks you through selecting products, enabling boosting, and running a boost campaign from the dashboard.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Shopee limits the number of products you can boost at a time. Check your Shopee seller dashboard for your current boost limits before selecting a large number of products.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product boosting page" icon="rocket" title-type="p">
    Navigate to the **Product Boosting** section in your dashboard. You will see a list of all your synced products with their current boost status.

    > 📸 **Screenshot:** Product boosting page showing the list of synced products and boost status column
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select products to boost" icon="check-square" title-type="p">
    Check the box next to each product you want to boost. You can select multiple products at once. A counter shows how many products you have selected.

    > 📸 **Screenshot:** Products selected with checkboxes and a selection counter
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable boosting for selected products" icon="toggle-right" title-type="p">
    After selecting products, click **Enable Boost** to mark them as eligible for the next boost campaign. You can also disable boosting for a product by unchecking it and clicking **Disable Boost**.

    > 📸 **Screenshot:** Enable Boost button highlighted with selected products shown
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run a boost campaign" icon="play" title-type="p">
    Click **Run Boost** to start the campaign. The platform sends your selected products to Shopee for boosting. Products are boosted in turns so that each one gets fair exposure, and no single product takes all the boost slots.

    > 📸 **Screenshot:** Run Boost button and campaign in-progress indicator
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review boosted and skipped products" icon="clipboard-check" title-type="p">
    After the campaign finishes, review the results in the boost status column. Successfully boosted products show a success indicator. Skipped products show a reason, usually because you exceeded Shopee's boost limit. Remove skipped products from your selection and try again in the next cycle.

    > 📸 **Screenshot:** Boost results table showing boosted products with success status and skipped products with reasons
  </Step>
</Steps>

Boosting can be run periodically to keep your products visible on Shopee. Check back after each campaign to see which products were boosted successfully and which need attention.
