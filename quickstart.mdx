## Get Started in Under a Minute

Follow this quickstart to generate your first redeem link from a Shopee order ID. Wally And Cody provides instant delivery through its auto-redeem system. You only need a valid Shopee order ID to get started—no accounts or signups required.

<Callout kind="info">

**Prerequisites:**

- A recent Shopee order ID (format: `YYMMDD` followed by letters/numbers, e.g., `231015ABC123`)
- Access to a web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- Stable internet connection

</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Guide

Use these steps to generate and use your redeem link. The entire process takes less than 60 seconds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Wally And Cody" icon="globe">
    Open your web browser and navigate to [https://wallyandcody.com](https://wallyandcody.com).

    You will see the simple interface with an "Order ID" input field and a "Get redeem link" button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Shopee Order ID" icon="hash">
    Locate the "Order ID" field.

    Paste or type your Shopee order ID. For example:

````plaintext
231015ABC123
````

    Order IDs are typically 9-12 characters starting with the order date (`YYMMDD`).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate the Redeem Link" icon="zap">
    Click the "Get redeem link" button.

    Wally And Cody processes the order ID instantly using its auto-redeem system. Within seconds, a redeem link appears.

    Copy the generated link to your clipboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Redeem Your Item" icon="link">
    Paste the redeem link into your browser or the relevant app/store.

    Follow the redemption prompts to claim your item. Check your email or Shopee account for confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify the Redemption Process

After generating the link, verify success across different devices.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop Browser" icon="monitor">
    Open the redeem link in a new tab. Log in to the partner platform if prompted. Confirm the item appears in your account.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Mobile App" icon="smartphone">
    Paste the link into your mobile browser or app store. Tap through the redemption flow. Check order history for updates.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Shopee App" icon="shopping-bag">
    Return to Shopee app > Orders > Track your order. Redeemed items show as "Claimed" or "Delivered".
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  If the link expires or fails, regenerate using the same order ID. Links are valid for a limited time.
</Callout>

## Common Troubleshooting

<Expandable title="What if my order ID is invalid?" default-open="true">
  Ensure the order ID matches exactly from your Shopee confirmation email or app (case-sensitive). Test with a recent order.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Link not working?">
  Clear browser cache, try incognito mode, or switch devices. Contact support via the website footer if issues persist.
</Expandable>
