## Seller and shop management

This guide walks you through setting up your seller account, linking your Shopee shop, and authorizing the platform to access your shop data.

<Callout kind="info">

There are two roles in the dashboard: **ADMIN** users can create seller accounts and manage all shops. **SELLER** users manage their own assigned shops and products. Ask your admin for access if you only have SELLER permissions.

</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Log in to the dashboard" icon="login">

Navigate to your dashboard URL in your browser. Enter your email and password, then click **Log In**.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Dashboard login page with email and password fields

</Step>

<Step title="Create a seller account" icon="user-plus">

This step requires an ADMIN account. Go to the **Seller & Shop Management** page and click **Add Seller**. Fill in the seller's name, email, and a temporary password. Set the role to **ADMIN** or **SELLER** based on what this person needs to do.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Add seller form with name, email, password, and role dropdown fields

</Step>

<Step title="Create and link a Shopee shop" icon="shopping-bag">

This step requires an ADMIN account. On the same Seller & Shop Management page, click **Add Shop**. Select the seller this shop belongs to, then enter your **Shopee Partner ID**, **Partner Key**, and **Shop ID**. These details come from your Shopee Open Platform developer settings.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Add shop form showing seller dropdown, partner ID, partner key, and shop ID fields

</Step>

<Step title="Authorize the shop with Shopee" icon="check-circle">

1. Find the shop you just created and click **Get Authorization URL**.
2. Open the URL in your browser. Log in to Shopee if prompted, then click **Approve**.
3. The platform automatically saves your access tokens. No manual step is needed. You should see a confirmation that authorization succeeded.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Authorization URL button and Shopee approval screen side by side

</Step>

<Step title="Check token health" icon="heart">

On the Seller & Shop Management page, look at the **Token Status** column for each shop. A green **Valid** status means you are connected and ready to go. If the status shows **Expired**, click **Re-authorize** to get a new authorization URL and repeat Step 4.

> 📸 **Screenshot:** Token health status column showing Valid and Expired states for different shops

</Step>
</Steps>

Once your shop is linked and authorized, you can sync products from Shopee, set up redemption URLs for buyers, and boost product visibility.
